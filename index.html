About Me

Creativity
Is My Passion

I'm Ibrahim, a video editor, motion designer and AI video creator from Egypt. I turn raw footage and rough ideas into videos that stop the scroll, hold attention and get the message across.

For close to three years I've been a freelancer, crafting promos, ads and social content for business owners and content creators in real estate, clinics, cars, restaurants and products. Along the way I've also worked with media agencies and production houses. In every project, editing, motion graphics and AI work as one toolkit, blending real footage, animated design and AI visuals into a single story.
